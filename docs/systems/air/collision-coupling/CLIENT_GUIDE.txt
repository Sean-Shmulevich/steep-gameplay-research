snow_game flight command consumer boundary

The documented flight controller publishes base velocity plus processed
environmental velocity. Its live branch adds position correction divided by dt
and copies the result unchanged into the actor component's selected movement
request. Actor construction supplies that request to the flight controller.
This object also receives an orientation update through a separate setter.

The next request-to-collision/body step remains unresolved. Treat the published
velocity as a command until a host movement backend resolves it. Its length is
not independent proof of actual travel speed after collision handling. This
supplement supplies no invented collision projection or direct rigid-body force
law for that boundary.

A separate pose/physical postprocess performs spatial queries, writes a result
vector into physical state, and conditionally enters a snow-runtime update.
That postprocess has not been joined to the flight movement request. Equal
numeric field offsets in separate objects do not establish the connection.

The bounded static search inspected nearby actor functions and full-image
vector-field candidates, then rejected several serialization/container and
pose-state matches. It does not prove that a consumer is absent elsewhere or
that indirect dispatch cannot connect the request. No new native equivalence
or complete gameplay result is claimed by this supplement.

WORLD, SKY AND WEATHER: DECODED DATA AND LIMITS

named_weather_settings.json is a self-contained record of the decoded
ENV_B_NEW_Foggy_01 season. It includes a named resource binding graph and actual
float32 sample values from its weather, wind and time-of-day controllers.

These are authoring-controller values, not claimed physical fog or wind units.

Documented binding graph

The season ENV_B_NEW_Foggy_01 selects:
  WorldAmbiance: WA_ENV_B_NEW_Foggy_01
  TimeOfDayPropertyControllerData: TOD_ENV_B_NEW_Foggy_01
  TimeOfDayPropertyControllerData: TOD_ENV_B_NEW_Foggy_01_MV
  WeatherPropertyControllerData: WEA_ENV_B_NEW_Foggy_01
  WindPropertyControllerData: GRN_SSN_SUNNY_Wind_CD
  TextureMap: SG_WORLD_AMBIANCE_REGION_B21_Map

WorldAmbiance selects LayeredSky LS_ENV_B_NEW_Foggy_01 and translucency settings
GRN_Test_Terrain. The sky's matte chain includes mesh SG_ENV_B_Matte_01_LOD0,
material SG_ENV_B_Matte_PerfectDay2_01, and its same-named texture set.
The complete named references are in the JSON. A season called Foggy therefore
uses a wind controller whose name contains SUNNY; naming alone cannot establish
an aerodynamic state, or that all weather appearance comes from a single asset.

Actual numeric controller samples

Each weather and wind entry resolves to an AnimTrackLinearFloat record. The
entry number is preserved as channel_number because terminal property names
have not been documented. Assigning such names as fog density, snowfall rate,
wind direction or wind speed would be unsupported.

All 21 weather channels have two equal samples. Their values, rounded only
for readability here, are:
  channel  1: 0.6       channel  2: 0        channel  3: 80
  channel  4: 0.01      channel  5: 40       channel  6: 50
  channel  7: 10        channel  8: 0.1      channel  9: 0
  channel 10: 1         channel 11: 0        channel 12: 0.03
  channel 13: 0.8       channel 14: 0.03     channel 15: 0.8
  channel 16: 0         channel 17: 100      channel 18: 50
  channel 19: 1         channel 20: 0.1      channel 21: 0.2

The JSON retains actual decoded float32 values, for example channel 1 is
0.5999999642372131 and channel 4 is 0.009999999776482582. Equal endpoints
make these channels constant under a linear interpolation between the samples:
(1-u)*a+u*a=a. This establishes their authored constant values within a segment;
it does not prove how different controllers are combined or their physical units.

The six wind channels contain sample pairs:
  1: 160 ->160
  2:   0 ->100
  3:   1 ->100
  4:   1 ->1
  5:   1 ->1
  6:   1 ->1
Their property paths include LayeredSky. A direct connection of these values
to rider aerodynamics has not been established, so they must not be interpreted
as metres/second without an additional consumer mapping.

Each time-of-day controller has 137 authored property entries. Of these, 128
have decoded linear-float sample arrays in the supplied JSON; the other nine
are not represented as floats. Some float tracks have three or more samples.
For example channel 1 is [0,360,0] in both variants. The number 360 is preserved
as data; its property has not been proven to be the sun angle.

The two time-of-day variants differ in seven decoded float channels: 59,110,
114,120,124,127,135. For example channel 114 in the ordinary resource has
[6.200000286102295,6.223072528839111], while the _MV resource has
[0.05347242206335068,0.05347242206335068]. The suffix _MV is preserved as an
asset name; its gameplay selection meaning is not inferred from that name.

Time encoding and curve playback

The serialized float track stores a sample count, one unsigned 16-bit interval
code per gap, then one float32 value per sample. Counts, interval arrays and
value arrays were checked to consume every selected track exactly, including
its final flags. The JSON preserves stored_interval_codes as integers.
An ordinary two-sample weather track commonly stores 12000, while some wind
tracks store 2880 or 10800. Their conversion into seconds, hours, normalized
weather blend or another clock has not been established. Do not treat these
integers as actual game time or assume uniform sample spacing.

A piecewise linear interpolation is the mathematical meaning of the identified
track type, but the actual playback clock, wrapping behavior, controller blend
order and property-path bindings still require consumer recovery. This folder
therefore provides real samples and exact named bindings rather than a fabricated
weather simulation or a renderer equation.

Fog and snow rendering

The included fog-update path hashes a 16-byte configuration block and rebuilds
its derived state when that hash changes. That establishes update invalidation;
it does not establish density, extinction, scattering, height falloff, volumetric
ray integration or the sky lookup-table equations. The presence of a sky asset
and its matte texture chain does not specify shader arithmetic.

No snowfall intensity, fog-density unit, scattering coefficient, snow albedo,
exposure, tone-mapping curve or shader implementation is supplied as a known
value. Reproducing the visual appearance needs the downstream property names,
selected materials/textures and shader consumers. The documented snow contact
and density-response physics in the companion guide do not imply snow rendering.

Validation

The decoded season contains 12 fully bounded named resources. Weather has
21/21 linear float tracks, wind 6/6, and each time-of-day resource 128 decoded
linear float tracks out of 137 entries. All included tracks satisfy their exact
serialized count/interval/value bounds. Unresolved field meanings remain explicit in the named JSON.
